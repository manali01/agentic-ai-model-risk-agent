MODEL NAME:
Credit Card Default Prediction Model

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
The model predicts the probability that a customer will default on credit card payments within the next 12 months. The model is used for account management, credit line adjustments, and customer risk monitoring.

MODEL METHODOLOGY:
The model uses logistic regression with regularization. Features include:
- Credit score
- Income
- Utilization ratio
- Delinquency history
- Number of open accounts
- Recent payment behavior
- Average monthly spend
- Geographic region

TRAINING DATA:
Training data includes customer records from January 2021 to December 2023. Data was collected from internal banking systems.

TARGET VARIABLE:
Whether a customer defaulted within 12 months.

VALIDATION PROCESS:
Validation includes train-test split validation and AUC measurement. Reported AUC is 0.78. KS statistic was also evaluated.

LIMITATIONS:
The documentation does not clearly mention out-of-time validation testing. Stability analysis across time periods is limited. Monitoring thresholds are not fully documented.

BIAS TESTING:
Limited discussion of fairness testing is included. Bias testing across demographic groups such as geography and income bands was not comprehensively performed.

FEATURE ENGINEERING:
Recent payment behavior was heavily weighted in feature engineering. Some derived features may overlap with future performance windows.

MONITORING:
Monthly monitoring is planned. However, escalation thresholds and challenger model criteria are not clearly documented.

MODEL GOVERNANCE:
Independent validation was performed internally. However, reviewer signoff documentation is incomplete.

IMPLEMENTATION:
The model is deployed using batch scoring every month. Outputs are stored in internal risk systems.

KNOWN RISKS:
Potential risks include:
- Data leakage
- Feature instability
- Population drift
- Bias against certain customer segments
- Weak monitoring controls

FUTURE ENHANCEMENTS:
Future work may include:
- Gradient boosting methods
- Enhanced bias testing
- Automated monitoring dashboards
- Improved challenger framework
